PriyaAI Logo
=============
The new PriyaAI logo is included under branding/PriyaAI_logo.png and
branding/PriyaAI_logo.svg.

For Android Studio, place the PNG into the app's drawable/mipmap resources
and set android:icon in AndroidManifest.xml to the corresponding mipmap
resource. Android Studio's Image Asset tool can generate all launcher
densities from the PNG.

The cloud-build workflow remains included in the project.
